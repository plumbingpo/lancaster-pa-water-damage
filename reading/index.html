Water Damage We See Most in Reading

Six kinds of calls make up the bulk of our work for Reading households. They are all common, and with every one of them waiting adds to the repair.

Mold: Mold is usually the second chapter of a water problem that someone thought was finished. Surfaces dry with towels and a box fan, yet the pad, subfloor and inside of the wall hold moisture. Since colonies can form in 24 to 48 hours, the first hint is often an earthy smell or speckled drywall weeks later.

Hoses: Every sink, toilet and washer is fed by a flexible connector that holds full house pressure around the clock. When one splits, it sprays until the water is turned off, which can mean hours if nobody is home. Replacing old connectors and knowing where your shutoff sits are the cheapest protection available.

Pumps: Sump pumps, where a home has one, tend to fail at the worst moment after years of running without attention. Stuck float switches, tripped breakers and burned-out motors are what we find most. The result is several inches of water over the lowest floor, soaking stored boxes, flooring and the bottom of the walls. Testing the pump now and then, and adding a battery backup, lowers the odds considerably.

Toilets: An overflowing toilet is the most common bathroom emergency we handle in Reading. Clean water from the tank or supply is one thing, but water from the bowl after use is contaminated. Either way it moves under tile edges and vanity cabinets, so the floor needs to be checked with a meter.

Cooling: Your air conditioner drains condensation through a line that most owners never see. A rusted pan or a plugged line lets that water run into nearby flooring or the ceiling below. The leak is gradual, so drywall and insulation may be soaked long before you spot a mark. Most of these are avoided by getting the drain cleaned annually ahead of heavy use.

Flooding: When storm drains cannot keep up with a downpour, surface water heads for the lowest doorway it can find. It reaches ground-level rooms, garages and walkout lower levels on the downhill side before anything else. Our Lancaster Restoration crews extract it, remove what cannot be cleaned and dry the structure to verified readings.
